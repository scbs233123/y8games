Shadowgun War Game

How to Play Shadowgun War Game

Shadowgun War Game is an intense tactical multiplayer FPS where strategy and quick reflexes are key to victory. Step into the arena, pick your favorite hero with unique abilities, team up with allies, and engage in high-octane gunfights across beautifully designed maps.

Controls & Gameplay

Use W, A, S, D to move and your Mouse to aim and shoot. Press Spacebar to jump, R to reload, and Shift to sprint. Use Q and E to activate your hero's special combat abilities.

Communicate with your team, control key areas of the map, and outsmart your opponents to secure the win!
