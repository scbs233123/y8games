How to Play Scary Granny

Welcome to Scary Granny! You are trapped inside a dark, mysterious house, and Granny is hunting for you. She hears everything, so you must stay quiet, hide under beds or inside wardrobes, solve various puzzles, and find key items to escape the house before time runs out.

Controls & Gameplay

Use W, A, S, D to move around, and use your Mouse to look around. Press E or left-click to interact with objects and open doors. On mobile devices, use on-screen touch controls to walk and interact.

Be extremely careful not to drop objects on the floor, as Granny will hear the noise and come running! Watch the video walkthrough below for helpful tips and puzzle solutions.
